Walkdown

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Where Walkdown runs

Walkdown is an Atlassian Forge app that runs entirely on Atlassian's platform ("Runs on Atlassian"). It does not send data to any external service, and it has no servers of its own.

What it stores

Walkdown stores rehearsal results in Forge app storage on your Atlassian site:

  • work item keys and summaries of the sampled requirements;
  • the trace status, findings, grades and readiness scores;
  • the Atlassian account ID of the person who started each rehearsal.

This data stays on your site, follows Atlassian's data residency, and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

What it reads and writes

Walkdown reads Jira work items, their links and fix versions, links to Confluence pages, and development information. It reads Teamwork Graph as the signed-in user, so it only sees what that user can see. It writes comments containing auditor questions. When you choose Load the GlucoLoop sample, it also creates a sample Jira project, two link types, a release, a Confluence space with design pages and sample development information. You can delete these like any other content.

AI

Walkdown's grading is rule-based and does not call AI models. The Walkdown Auditor agent runs in Atlassian Rovo, under your organisation's Rovo service and terms.

Cookies and tracking

Walkdown sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. Atlassian may collect platform logs and metrics under its own policies.
