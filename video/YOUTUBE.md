## Title

Walkdown: rehearse your medical-device software audit inside Jira (Codegeist 2026)

## Description

Walkdown is a practice auditor for teams building medical-device software. It lives inside Jira, samples requirements the way an auditor would (riskiest first), follows each one from design to code, test, risk control and release, and stops at the first broken link. Every finding is graded major, minor or observation, cites the clause of IEC 62304, ISO 14971, ISO 13485 or 21 CFR 820.30, and asks the question an auditor would ask. A readiness score out of 100 shows progress from release to release.

Use it from the Walkdown page in Jira, assign it a ticket like a teammate, ask for a rehearsal in plain words on a work item, chat with it in Rovo, or call it from any AI tool through Atlassian's MCP server. A workflow validator stops a requirement with a broken trace from being released.

Grading is fixed rules, not AI guesswork: the same records always give the same result. It runs on Atlassian Forge, and no data leaves your site.

The project shown is GlucoLoop, a fictional insulin-dosing app with a few traces broken on purpose. Walkdown is a rehearsal tool, not a formal audit opinion.
